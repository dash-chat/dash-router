Dash Router demos

Recorded simulations of Dash Router spreading messages across a network. Press play, or step with the arrows.

  1. 1. A line of five One writer at the end of a line, no loss. A Want travels out, the Have comes back.
  2. 2. A small LAN Twenty nodes, four writers, 2% loss. Many timers at once, Wants suppressing each other.
  3. 3. A lossy mesh Fifty nodes, six writers, 15% of messages lost. Repair traffic keeps everyone in sync.

Each node carries small pie timers: amber counts down to its next Want (asking for what it is missing), blue to its next Have (answering with what it holds). Edges light up in the same colours while those messages are in flight.
